# SHRUNK

A 3D third-person browser game. Experiment 7 went wrong: you're a 1 cm scientist, a crow dropped you in Central Park, and you have to get back to the lab.

**Play:** https://blackhawk12222.github.io/shrunk/

## Controls
- Desktop: WASD move, mouse look (click to lock), Space jump, Shift sprint, Click/F attack, Esc pause
- Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons for Jump / Hit / Run

## What's in it
- Streamed procedural world in chunks: Central Park lawn and pond, sidewalk, W 59th St, north sidewalk, alley, lab
- Context-driven prop placement and resident profiles that vary each block
- Animated water with ripples, wind-blown grass, dust motes
- Ants (more near food), a spider guarding the alley, crow dives (hide under cover), sneaker stomps (get out of the red)
- Dew drops and crumbs heal; a toothpick spear doubles damage; checkpoints

Built with three.js (bundled in `lib/`, no build step).
